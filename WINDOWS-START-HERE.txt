ROCKFORM 0.1.8 - WINDOWS PROTOTYPE

START THE APP
1. Obtain Rockform-0.1.8-Windows-x64.zip.
2. Use Extract All to unpack the entire ZIP.
3. Open the extracted folder and double-click Rockform.exe.

Keep Rockform.exe and the _internal folder together. Do not run the app from
inside the ZIP. Python, Docker, and development tools are not needed.
This is a portable app: there is no installer and no system-wide installation.
The executable is not code-signed; Windows may identify its publisher as unknown.

FIRST USE
Try a synthetic sample from the left-hand sidebar, or import an OBJ/STL scan.
Check its dimensions. Scaniverse OBJ files that declare meters are converted
automatically; still compare the dimensions with a measured reference.

1 Repair: Paint area of interest, Preview selected area, then Apply selected area.
Keep surrounding wall (20 mm margin initially), or use the entire current scan.
Next: Preview repair & cleanup, inspect the changes, then Apply preview.
2 Holds: Suggest holds, choose a result, Preview hold, then Keep hold.
3 Mounts: Try the included bolt or screw profile, generate mounts, and preview
the cuts. Check the preset dimensions against your actual hardware before printing.
Export STL for your slicer in millimeters. Save project to keep editable work.

The Guide button explains the workflow. Right-drag paints with a selected brush;
left-drag rotates the view and the mouse wheel zooms.

NEW IN 0.1.8
Raised holds retain 10 mm of surrounding scanned rock for fingers wrapping
around the base. In 2 Holds, adjust Surrounding rock from 0 to 50 mm before
suggesting or previewing. Retained texture, overhangs, and grip surfaces stay on
the working scan. A 3 mm backing and shallow outside return close the shape.
Set 0 mm for the previous root-only extraction with no added backing.
For an existing hold, Re-extract natural feature makes a new preview while
keeping the saved design and its holes. Recess extraction is unchanged.
If the margin reaches missing scan data, repair/rescan or reduce the margin.

IMPORT AND AREA SELECTION
Import defaults to reducing dense, low-detail areas after scale confirmation.
The original stays intact; Undo or Reset restores it. Uncheck the import option
to retain every triangle. Import does not smooth or repair gaps.
Paint an area of interest before repair to exclude unwanted broken scan edges.
Preview first, then apply. Crop edges stay open. Undo restores excluded areas.
Selections, unfinished crop previews and margins survive saving and reopening.

QUICK REPAIR
Select a raised feature marked Needs repair, then use 2 Holds > Quick repair.
Fill down to wall adds material below the observed surface without a plate.
Trim damaged base cuts off the lower damaged portion; adjust its height if needed.
Click Preview quick repair, compare the original in coral, then Keep hold.
Amber shows added material. Fill can cover undercuts; trim makes the hold shallower.
Restore original feature remains available after saving and reopening.
Both options must pass solid checks before mounting/export. Recesses still use
scan gap repair to preserve their cavity. Missing front patches may need a rescan.
Projects keep previews, selections, settings and the saved view. New saves use
format 8 and require Rockform 0.1.7 or later; older projects remain readable.

A reference Scaniverse scan yields a roughly 171 x 130 x 42 mm raised hold with
the default contact margin. Match the editable prototype profiles to your
actual hardware before printing.

TESTING STATUS
Tested on the development Windows 11 x64 computer, including the standalone
runtime, 3D viewer, synthetic examples, and supplied real scans. Other PCs,
Windows versions, graphics drivers, slicers, and physical prints need independent
testing. The application requires a working OpenGL-capable graphics driver.

Outputs are shape and mounting prototypes, not qualified climbing equipment.
The iPhone capture companion is a separate source project, not part of this ZIP.
