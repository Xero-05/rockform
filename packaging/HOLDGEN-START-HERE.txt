ROCKFORM HOLD GENERATOR - WINDOWS PROTOTYPE

START THE APP
1. Use Extract All to unpack the entire ZIP.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click RockformHoldgen.exe.

Keep RockformHoldgen.exe and the _internal folder together. Do not run the app
from inside the ZIP. Python and development tools are not needed. This is a
portable app: there is no installer. The executable is not code-signed;
Windows may identify its publisher as unknown.

USE
Pick a hold type and size, set the difficulty, wall angle and rotation, or type
a description such as "hard 2-finger pocket on a 30 degree overhang" or
"2 inch crimp". Click the pull chart or a grab style to see the difficulty for
that pull. Export writes an STL in millimeters plus a JSON report.

ROUTES
The Route tab sets whole boulder routes of generated holds on a wall. Set the
wall, pick a grade (V0-V8) and click Add route to wall. Each hold is drilled for
its bolt, turned so its best grip faces the climber's pull, and rated as placed.
Drag holds in the 3D view to move them bolt to bolt (mouse wheel or Q / E turns
them while dragging); the route is re-rated when you drop them. Inspect in hold
view opens a hold in the Hold tab. Export wall writes the STLs, reports, a
route.svg per route and a layout.json for the Blender Rock Wall Builder add-on.
The estimated grade is a prototype heuristic, not calibrated on climbers.

HOLDGEN.md (inside _internal) explains how difficulty is rated. The score is a
holding-area proxy, not a climbing grade.

SELF-TEST
RockformHoldgen.exe --self-test <folder>
generates and validates one hold of each type, then plans, drills, validates
and exports one V3 route, into <folder>.
